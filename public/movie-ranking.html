Bryan's Favourite Movies

My top 5 movies of all time.

Perfect Days (2023)

I love how calm and simple this movie is. It makes everyday life feel beautiful.

Touch (2024)

I love the emotional story and how it shows love that lasts even after many years.

Rental Family (2025)

I love the heartwarming story and how it shows the importance of human connection.

Meet Joe Black (1998)

I love the unique story about love, life, and death. It is emotional andmemorable.

18×2 Beyond Youthful Days (2024)

I love the nostalgic feeling and the beautiful journey through Japan. The story about first love is very touching.
